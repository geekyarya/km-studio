## Launch grid: post these 9 before following anyone
An empty profile doesn't convert visitors to followers, so post all 9 in the first 2–3 days (the top row ends up being posts 7–9).
1. Intro reel: "Hi, main Kiara 👋" while walking at Marine Drive at sunset
2. Saree look on a Bandra rooftop
3. Café photo with chai, candid laugh
4. Gym mirror clip
5. Jaipur throwback: Hawa Mahal, pastel lehenga
6. Rainy window and chai, soft mood
7. Night-out look in Bandra, black dress
8. POV reel: "POV: you meet me at a Mumbai wedding"
9. Outfit poll: "Saree or gown? 1 or 2?"

Generate these by running the pipeline with a fixed plan, or by writing the prompts by hand with `persona.LOOK`.

## Settings checklist
- [ ] Sign up with a new email (for example kiara.mehra.creator@gmail.com), not your personal Instagram
- [ ] Settings → Account type → switch to **Creator** account, category "Digital creator"
- [ ] Settings → link to a new Facebook Page called "Kiara Mehra" (needed for the auto-posting API)
- [ ] Turn on the **"AI info"** label on posts and reels
- [ ] Set the account to Public
- [ ] Warm up for 3–5 days before posting heavily: browse Reels and like posts in the fashion and Mumbai niches. A brand-new account that posts a lot right away looks like a bot.

## First 30 days
- Post 1 Reel a day (the pipeline does this) and 2–3 Stories a day (polls, "this or that", questions)
- Reply to every comment in Kiara's voice within the first hour after posting
- Comment in character on 10–15 posts a day from mid-size Indian fashion and lifestyle creators
- Hashtag mix: 3 large (#mumbaigirl #indianfashion #sareelove), 5 medium, 2 small or niche, plus #AIgenerated
